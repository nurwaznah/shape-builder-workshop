🔎 Prism or Non-Prism?

PRISM

  • 2 matching bases
  • Flat faces
  • The same shape continues along its length

NON-PRISM

  • Does not have 2 matching bases connected by flat faces
  • May have a curved surface
  • Faces may meet at a point
